What NAVUMSHA is

A playground for things that fly

When you throw a ball, kick a football or fire a cannon, the object follows a curved path. That path is called projectile motion. NAVUMSHA lets you build that path yourself, then look inside it.

Pick a speed, an angle and a planet. Press launch. Watch the numbers, the arrows and the graphs, and see exactly why the object went where it did.

What you can explore

Eight ways to throw the world

Each mode sets up a new idea. You can still change every slider afterwards.

MODE 1

Ideal (no air)

The classic textbook parabola. Only gravity acts. See why 45° gives the longest range on flat ground.

Try it in the lab
MODE 2

Horizontal launch from a cliff

Fire straight out and watch gravity pull the path down while the sideways speed stays the same.

Try it in the lab
MODE 3

Launch from a height

Start high and the best angle drops below 45°. Find out how much.

Try it in the lab
MODE 4

Raised or lower landing level

Aim onto a platform above you or a pit below you, and see how the best angle moves.

Try it in the lab
MODE 5

Air resistance

Add drag. The path is no longer a parabola and the range shrinks a lot at high speed.

Try it in the lab
MODE 6

Wind

Choose speed and direction. A tailwind helps, a headwind hurts, a crosswind pushes you off line.

Try it in the lab
MODE 7

Spin and the Magnus effect

Backspin lifts, topspin dives, sidespin curves. Spin a golf ball or a football and see it bend.

Try it in the lab
MODE 8

Other planets

Moon, Mars, Venus and Jupiter. Same throw, very different flight.
